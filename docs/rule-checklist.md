# Financial Statements Tie-Out rule checklist

This independently written first version checks configured numerical relationships. It is not a complete US GAAP disclosure checklist, an accounting-policy assessment, or an audit opinion. Rules must be adapted to the engagement's captions, scope and presentation. No external project's code has been copied.

The executable catalog is `rules/core.json`: 35 rules with stable IDs, explicit signed terms and descriptions. The engine also generates TB balance, mapping completeness, mapping confirmation and one comparison for each mapped FSLI. A missing fact is never silently interpreted as zero.

## Reading and adapting the checklist

- Each fact uses the engagement's entity, currency and period. `PY.` keys explicitly identify prior-period operands. Do not mix annual, year-to-date and quarterly figures.
- Facts are multiplied by their positive `unit` before calculation. For example, a value of `300` with unit `1000` represents 300,000 currency units.
- TB balances use debit positive and credit negative. Each mapping has an explicit presentation sign. Under this sample template, liabilities, revenue and most expenses are positive presentation amounts, while cash flows and net nonoperating movements retain their economic sign.
- Components must be complete and disjoint. Do not include both a subtotal and its children in the same sum. An absent category requires a verified explicit zero or an adapted rule, not an inferred zero.
- Basis must match, especially for consolidated versus parent income, NCI, gross versus net debt, and carrying amount versus undiscounted lease commitments. A fact's `basis` can flag incompatible scope; the reviewer must also validate the economic meaning of the selected captions.
- The cash template includes restricted cash in both current and noncurrent captions. FASB's [ASU 2016-18, Statement of Cash Flows: Restricted Cash](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU_2016-18.pdf), amendments to ASC 230-10-45-4 and 230-10-50-8, explains the inclusion and reconciliation of restricted cash with the cash-flow totals. This is a numeric implementation reference, not a substitute for the applicable Codification or engagement review.

## TB checks generated by the engine

| ID | Check | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TB-01 | Sum signed TB balances = 0 | Verify debit/credit balance in base currency units. |
| TB-02 | Every nonzero TB account has a mapping | Unmapped exposure uses absolute balances, so offsetting omissions cannot cancel. Monetary tolerance cannot waive incomplete mapping. |
| TB-03 | Mappings have explicit reviewer confirmation | Unconfirmed mappings cannot produce passing bridge comparisons. |
| TB-FSLI:&lt;fact key&gt; | Sum account balances × configured sign = reported FSLI | Supports many accounts to one FSLI; split mapping is intentionally unsupported. Preserve contributing accounts in the bridge. |

## Applicability decisions

| Condition | Used by | Required reviewer decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scf_indirect_method` | FS-04 | The cash-flow statement's income reconciliation starts with the same income measure. |
| `equity_income_scope_matches` | FS-05, M-14 | Income, dividends and retained earnings have the same attribution scope. Adjust the formula for NCI or other allocations where required. |
| `da_scope_matches` | FS-09 | D&A includes the same expense population on both sides; consider cost-of-revenue presentation, capitalization and other reconciliation items. |
| `sbc_scope_matches` | FS-10 | Equity and cash-flow SBC amounts cover the same population and period; consider liability awards, capitalization and other adjustments. |
| `ppe_gross_net_components_confirmed` | N-11 | The configured gross PPE includes all components included in net PPE, and accumulated depreciation is a positive magnitude subtracted by this formula. Confirm how construction in progress (CIP), impairment and any other valuation accounts are included. |

Set a condition to `true` only after confirming the scope. `false` produces `NOT_APPLICABLE`; absent or `null` produces `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Document the rationale in the settings reason. A rule can separately be marked not applicable with a rule-specific reason. Neither a missing note nor a missing amount establishes non-applicability.

For N-11, configure the formula and input facts for the engagement before confirming the condition. If accumulated depreciation is supplied as a signed negative contra balance, its coefficient must reflect that sign convention. If net PPE includes separately presented CIP not included in the selected gross amount, add the appropriate CIP term; likewise configure impairment or other adjustments explicitly. Do not infer a sign reversal, assume omitted components are zero, or confirm the predicate solely because the supplied amounts happen to tie.

## Automatic note matching by content

The tool does not assume that PPE is Note 3 or that tax is Note 11. Note numbers and row IDs serve only as evidence references. The matcher configuration in `rules/note_matchers.json` identifies 12 semantic values using English and Chinese topic/caption aliases, including simplified and traditional Chinese where configured.

Automatic matching requires both a topic phrase in the note title/body and a normalized caption alias. Punctuation, casing and common fullwidth characters are normalized. Captions use conservative exact aliases: a line such as “Accrued expenses - payroll” is not accepted as the total accrued-expense caption merely because it contains similar words. Aliases are editable for an engagement; a reviewer can also make an explicit selection.

Gross/net and current/noncurrent captions are kept separate. Amount similarity is never used to select a source. Matching an amount merely because it equals the statement would hide the very discrepancies this tool is meant to find.

Structured note input has the following form; `period`, `currency` and `basis` are required on every row and must match the engagement. An explicitly supplied entity at the note or row level must also match; when absent, the engagement entity is implied.

```json
{
  "notes": [
    {
      "id": "Note 27",
      "title": "Property, plant and equipment",
      "rows": [
        {
          "id": "net-carrying",
          "label": "Property, plant and equipment, net",
          "value": "285",
          "unit": "1000",
          "source": "draft-financial-statements.xlsx",
          "location": "Notes!D42",
          "period": "FY2026",
          "currency": "USD",
          "basis": "consolidated"
        }
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

The `period` above is illustrative: use the exact engagement-period string in the actual input. Decimal amounts must be strings or exact integers; floating-point values are rejected. Missing amounts remain `null` and are never filled with zero.

| Match status | Result |
| --- | --- |
| AUTO_MATCHED | Exactly one content candidate has the required scope. Source and row lineage are retained. |
| MANUAL_MATCHED | The reviewer explicitly selected an eligible source row and recorded a reason. |
| AMBIGUOUS | Multiple candidates exist, or a generic row could populate multiple distinct semantic targets. Affected checks require review. |
| SCOPE_MISMATCH | A candidate has missing/conflicting period, currency, basis or explicit entity metadata. Affected checks require review. |
| NOT_FOUND | No eligible content candidate was found. Affected checks are not tested. |

This first version deliberately requires a reviewed selection when competing candidates include different periods, even if one matches the engagement. A period column must not be guessed. An override can select a correctly scoped current-period row while leaving the alternative candidates visible.

Explicit selections are recorded under `settings.note_overrides`, with the existing settings reviewer identifying the person recording the decision:

```json
{
  "note_overrides": {
    "NOTE.ppe_net": {
      "note_id": "Note 27",
      "row_id": "net-carrying",
      "reason": "Confirmed current-period net PPE caption against the source."
    }
  }
}
```

An override may select a caption outside the aliases after review, but cannot bypass missing or conflicting scope or missing provenance. Unknown source identifiers and blank reasons are rejected. When structured notes are supplied, existing manually keyed facts for the same note targets are rejected rather than silently overwritten. Existing manually keyed note facts remain supported when no `notes` input is supplied.

Each review stores the raw notes input, matcher configuration/hash, candidate lists, selection method and derived facts separately. Renumbering and reordering notes, including using a different company with different numbering, leaves the semantic matches and comparison values unchanged when the underlying content/scope is unchanged. Direct PDF/Word extraction is a separate pending capability; this version accepts structured note tables and does not claim to parse uploaded drafts automatically.

## Tolerances and outcomes

Default global tolerance is zero. The global amount and each rule override must be finite decimal magnitudes between 0 and 50,000 inclusive, in the engagement's base currency. The displayed ± range is symmetric; enter a nonnegative magnitude. A rule override of zero takes precedence over a nonzero global amount. Monetary tolerances do not waive mapping completeness or confirmation.

The calculation is `signed difference = normalized left side − normalized right side`:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| EXACT_MATCH | The numerical difference is exactly zero. |
| WITHIN_TOLERANCE | The difference is nonzero and its absolute amount is no greater than the effective tolerance. The difference remains an exception for review. |
| FAIL | The numerical difference exceeds tolerance, or a required structural check fails. |
| REVIEW_REQUIRED | Applicability, scope or mapping confirmation requires reviewer action. |
| NOT_TESTED | Required evidence is absent; this is never a pass. |
| NOT_APPLICABLE | A documented applicability decision excludes the rule; it remains visible in the audit trail. |

No rounding allowance is silently added. Rounding policy and materiality are distinct from this numerical tolerance. Do not total differences across rules as a misstatement amount: multiple rules may identify the same underlying issue.

## Synthetic demonstration

`examples/demo.json` is invented data, not a real client's financial statement. Its source labels and cell references are logical fixture locations; they do not refer to an attached original workbook. The six-account closing TB is balanced and maps to six balance-sheet FSLIs. It is deliberately a closing balance-sheet TB, so it does not demonstrate revenue/expense TB mapping. The 64 statement facts and 12 structured note rows use a unit of 1,000; the TB uses base USD. The note rows are grouped into seven illustrative notes. All 12 semantic note values are automatically matched from their content; none is manually keyed into the facts list.

The scenario has:

- Cash of 100,000, receivables of 200,000, PPE of 300,000; liabilities of 150,000 and equity of 450,000.
- Restricted cash explicitly zero for current, noncurrent and prior-period balances.
- Net income of 50,000 and dividends of 10,000, taking opening equity of 410,000 to closing equity of 450,000.
- A deliberate PPE note difference: balance sheet 300,000 versus note 285,000. The note's own gross-to-net arithmetic is internally consistent at 400,000 − 115,000 = 285,000.
- An unconfirmed D&A scope and an explicitly inapplicable SBC comparison to demonstrate that applicability is separate from matching numbers.

The optional top-level `demo_settings` is a ready-to-load example configuration; production defaults remain zero. It sets global tolerance to 10,000 and the FS-01 cash override to zero, with reviewer and reason recorded. Using those settings produces 44 results: 41 `EXACT_MATCH`, one `FAIL` (N-01, difference +15,000), one `REVIEW_REQUIRED` (FS-09) and one `NOT_APPLICABLE` (FS-10). There are 42 numerically tested results and three exception rows. At global tolerance 15,000 exactly, N-01 changes to `WITHIN_TOLERANCE`; it stays in the exception list. At 14,999.99 it remains `FAIL`.

## Audit evidence and current scope

A review preserves both original and normalized operands, source references, formula, signed and absolute difference, effective tolerance and origin, applicability reason, reviewer settings, and input/rule snapshots with hashes. Hashes support reproducibility; they are not a digital signature or proof of reviewer identity.

This version operates on supplied structured facts and confirmed mappings. It does not certify extraction accuracy, disclosure completeness, accounting classifications, consolidation eliminations, journal-entry support, or the completeness of the selected caption list. Prior-year restatements, special equity classes and nonstandard presentations require explicit configuration. A favorable numeric result does not resolve those review matters.
